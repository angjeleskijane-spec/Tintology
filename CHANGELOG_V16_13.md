# Tintology Studio V16.13 — Tint Scale & Clearer Glass

Based on V16.12.

## Changes
- Preserved the improved glass-tint ordering and transparency behavior from V16.12.
- Remapped the previous 95% near-black appearance to the new 85% stop.
- Added 100% as the new darkest, near-black setting.
- Reduced the pale/white cast in the tint color stops, especially at the lighter settings.
- Kept the five approved vehicle models and the Wrap simulator/catalog unchanged.

## Tint scale
- 12% — lightest smoke tint
- 50% — medium tint
- 65% — darker tint
- 85% — previous V16.12 95% appearance
- 100% — new darkest setting

Percentages are simulator darkness levels, not physical VLT values.

## Validation
- Source changes and archive contents checked.
- Production build and live Sketchfab material validation were not run in this environment.
